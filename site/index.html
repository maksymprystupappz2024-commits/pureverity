How much does recurring cleaning cost?

Every home is different. We provide an individual fixed price per visit based on the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, the cleaning frequency, and your home's needs. Send a quote request to discuss your plan.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No. Our team brings its own supplies and equipment. Let us know about any product preferences, sensitivities, or surfaces needing special care before we visit.

Can I add window or carpet cleaning?

Interior window cleaning and carpet vacuuming can be added to recurring visits by agreement. Carpet vacuuming is not a steam-cleaning or carpet extraction service. These are not offered as standalone bookings.

Do you clean homes with children or pets?

Yes, we work with families and homes with pets. Tell us about your household, pets, and any special instructions when requesting your quote so we can discuss your needs.

What happens after I request a quote?

We'll contact you to discuss your home, check your service address, and agree on a price and schedule. Submitting a request does not book a visit or take a payment.
